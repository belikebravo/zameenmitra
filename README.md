# ZameenMitra

## Abstract

ZameenMitra is an end-to-end digital platform designed to simplify and secure property transactions in India by combining document management, legal verification, and transaction workflow management into a single system.

The platform enables buyers, sellers, and legal professionals to upload and organize property documents, perform structured verification of ownership and legal status, and manage the entire transaction lifecycle through a transparent, step-by-step workflow. By reducing reliance on fragmented processes, manual verification, and intermediaries, ZameenMitra aims to improve trust, reduce fraud, and accelerate property transactions.

The system can integrate with existing government record systems such as Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme and state portals, while providing a user-friendly workflow layer on top.

## Feature List

### 1. Property Document Vault
* Upload property-related documents (single or bulk)
* Structured organization:
    * Ownership documents (Sale Deed, Title Deed)
    * Government records (Khata, Patta, RTC)
    * Financial documents (Loan papers, NOCs)
* Document preview and metadata extraction
* Persistent storage for future transactions

### 2. Smart Document Processing
* Auto-classification of uploaded documents
* Key data extraction:
    * Owner name
    * Survey number
    * Property details
* OCR support for scanned documents
* Duplicate detection and version tracking

### 3. Legal Verification Engine (Core USP)
* Ownership chain validation (historical records)
* Encumbrance and liability checks
* Detection of inconsistencies:
    * Missing ownership links
    * Name mismatches
* Risk flagging system
* Property “Verification Status” (Clear / Risk / Needs Review)

### 4. Property Risk Score
* Computed score based on:
    * Document completeness
    * Ownership clarity
    * Legal issues
* Simple visual indicator for buyers
* Detailed breakdown of risk factors

### 5. Transaction Workflow Management
* End-to-end transaction tracking:
    * Property shortlisted
    * Token amount paid
    * Agreement created
    * Loan processing
    * Registration completed
* Multi-party workflow:
    * Buyer
    * Seller
    * Lawyer
    * Bank
* Checklist-driven progress tracking

### 6. Secure Collaboration & Sharing
* Share property documents securely with stakeholders
* Role-based access (buyer/seller/lawyer)
* Controlled permissions (view/edit/comment)
* Replace email/WhatsApp document exchange

### 7. Digital Signing & Approvals
* Integration-ready digital signature support
* Agreement approval workflows
* Support for services like Aadhaar eSign

### 8. Property Timeline & Audit Trail
* Chronological view of:
    * Ownership history
    * Document updates
    * Verification steps
* Full audit log of actions
* Transparency for all stakeholders

### 9. Notifications & Alerts
* Updates on verification status
* Alerts for missing or incorrect documents
* Transaction milestone notifications

### 10. Security & Compliance
* Secure document storage
* Role-based authentication and authorization
* Data encryption (in transit & at rest)
* Audit-ready logs

### 11. Optional (Future Scope)
* Integration with state land record portals
* Auto-fetch property records
* Lawyer marketplace
* Loan/bank integrations
* Integration with platforms like NoBroker for discovery layer
